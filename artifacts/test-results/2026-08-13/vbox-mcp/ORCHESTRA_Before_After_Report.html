Identity cookie was also wrong before: userspace /proc starttime (ticks) was compared to p->start_boottime (ns). After, cookie 0 means “do not check,” so a published RUN/MIGRATE can match the opted-in PID.

7. Still open

  • SLEEP / THROTTLE remain min-slice approximations (scx_bpf_consume previously failed on this 6.12 path).
  • Completion-time cost under ENQ_LAST is uncalibrated (n=1, VirtualBox).
  • No bare-metal, no NUMA, no repeated statistical protocol (WP7).
  • cgroup cpu.weight dmesg warning remains (same class as scx_simple).
  • Bridge maps are still local-trust only (no cryptographic authentication).

Publication wording: “the VirtualBox guest demonstrated owned SCHED_EXT placement after opt-in” — not deployment-ready, not universally faster, not a paper reproduction of userspace Q(t).
